Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

What you type never leaves your browser

This tool runs entirely in your browser. Converting romanization to Korean is a purely client-side computation: there is no server and no database. Nothing you type is transmitted, logged, or seen by us.

Accounts and personal data

There is no login. Apart from what you choose to send through Feedback, we do not collect names, email addresses, or any other personally identifying data.

Feedback

When you send something with the Feedback button at the bottom right of each page, your message, the page you were on, your screen size and browser version, and the email address you choose to leave (if any) are emailed to the site owner through Cloudflare. Nothing is stored in a database, and it is used only to improve the site and reply to you. Text you type in the tool is not included.

Analytics

This site may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall traffic. It is cookieless, does not track individual users or behavior across sites, and only provides aggregated, anonymous numbers.

Advertising

This site may show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can adjust or turn off personalized ads at Google Ads Settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@latype.com.
