Romanization Converter

Read it aloud before you can read the script. Paste text in another script, get romanization the way it is pronounced, and practise line by line.

  • Japanese to Romaji ありがとう arigatou
  • Chinese to Pinyin 我愛你 wǒ ài nǐ
  • Thai to Romanization สวัสดี sa-wat-di

How to use it

  1. Pick a language and paste your text. The romanization appears below as you type.
  2. To practise sentence by sentence, paste a whole passage (a lesson, a dialogue or song lyrics) and turn on Line by line: each original line is followed by its romanization.
  3. Each language has its own options: Korean can follow pronunciation or spelling, Chinese lets you choose how tones are shown, Japanese how long vowels are written, and Thai whether syllables are split with hyphens.
